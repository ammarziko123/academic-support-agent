
# Attendance Policy

## 1. Attendance Requirements

Students enrolled in sample academy courses are expected to attend scheduled sessions and participate in learning activities.

For this fictional academy, students must attend at least 80% of the scheduled sessions to qualify for the standard course-completion certificate.

The attendance percentage is calculated as:

Attendance Percentage = (Sessions Attended / Total Scheduled Sessions) * 100

Example:
A student attends 14 out of 16 sessions.

Attendance Percentage = (14 / 16) * 100 = 87.5%

The student satisfies the sample attendance requirement because 87.5% is greater than 80%.

## 2. Late Arrival

A student is considered late when arriving more than 10 minutes after the scheduled session start time.

For this sample policy, three late arrivals are counted as one absence for attendance calculations.

Students should notify the instructor if they expect to arrive late. Instructors may record the actual arrival time.

## 3. Absence Procedure

Students who cannot attend a session should notify the academy support team before the session whenever possible.

The notification should include:
- Student name or academy student ID
- Course name
- Session date
- Reason for absence, if the student wishes to provide it

Submitting an absence notification does not automatically convert an absence into an attended session.

Students who experience an emergency should contact the support team to ask whether an exception can be reviewed.

## 4. Course Completion

Under this sample policy, students must meet all of the following conditions to qualify for the standard course-completion certificate:

- Attend at least 80% of scheduled sessions.
- Submit all mandatory assignments.
- Complete the required final project or assessment, when applicable.
- Meet any additional completion requirements explicitly stated in the relevant course document.

Meeting the attendance requirement alone does not guarantee a certificate.

## 5. Attendance Records and Disputes

Students should review their attendance records regularly.

If a student believes an attendance entry is incorrect, they should contact the academy support team and provide the course name, session date, and a description of the discrepancy.

The support team should review the available records before confirming any correction.

## 6. Frequently Asked Attendance Questions

### Does an absence notification count as attendance?

No. Sending a notification does not automatically count as attending the session.

### Can a student with 75% attendance receive the standard certificate?

Not under the default sample requirement of 80%. The student may contact support to ask whether an exception process exists, but no exception is guaranteed.

### Does attending every session guarantee course completion?

No. Mandatory assignments and other stated course requirements must also be completed.
